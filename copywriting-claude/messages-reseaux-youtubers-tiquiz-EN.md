## 🎯 Platform rules

### Twitter/X
- 100 words max
- 2 links max (demo + sales page if really needed)
- Direct, casual tone
- Reference a **tweet or a thread** of theirs, not a video
- No response in 7 days: drop it

### Instagram
- 1-2 emojis max
- Reference a **reel, a post or a story** of theirs
- Voice note 30-45 seconds works well if you already follow each other
- No response in 5 days: react to one of their stories, then follow up 3 days later

### LinkedIn
- Connection request first with a note
- DM within 24h of acceptance
- Reference a **post or an article** of theirs
- No "I hope this message finds you well"
- No response in 10 days: follow-up + engage with their posts

### Threads / Bluesky
- 60-80 words max
- Reference a **post** of theirs
- 1 link max
- Casual, near-spoken tone

---

## 🚫 Never write in a DM

- ❌ "I hope this message finds you well"
- ❌ "I'd love to collaborate with you"
- ❌ "Our revolutionary solution"
- ❌ "You'll make a lot of money"
- ❌ "I'm reaching out on behalf of..."
- ❌ Stats or sources (this is a DM, not an article)
- ❌ Referencing a video if the person doesn't make YouTube content
- ❌ Emoji chains 🚀💰🎯 (spam signature)
- ❌ bit.ly / tinyurl shortened links (spam signature)
- ❌ Calling them "influencer" or "creator economy expert"

## 📝 Variables to fill in

- `[FIRST NAME]` : double-checked on their bio
