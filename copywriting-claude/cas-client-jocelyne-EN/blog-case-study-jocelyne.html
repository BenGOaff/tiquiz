Pivoting to a new niche, from scratch

Jocelyne Bacquet spent 40 years working as a speech therapist focused on neurodevelopmental disorders. She also wrote — a lot: about 70 novels published before this new chapter.

In 2026, during a coaching session on writing a non-fiction niche book, she decided, in her own words, "to put my 40 years of work as a speech therapist on neurodevelopmental disorders to use, to help people ease the impact of their condition."

At launch, her Facebook and Instagram accounts dedicated to this new project had just been created. No existing audience, no email list. The book launched on Amazon — but with no one to read it.

Rather than waiting to build an organic audience, Jocelyne chose another path: a Tiquiz quiz to qualify her future readers, and a Meta ads campaign run by Gwenn to bring in the traffic.

The Tiquiz quiz: "What kind of ADHD brain do you have?"

Why this specific topic? In Jocelyne's own words: "It's a hot topic right now, but most books out there bring nothing new or are AI-written." Her edge: 40 years of clinical experience in neurodevelopment. A quiz where every question, every option, every result is rooted in real expertise.

1

5 questions, 5 tailored profiles

Five multiple-choice questions, five possible profiles at the end. Each profile describes how the respondent's brain works, their natural strengths, their daily friction points, and offers concrete ways to ease what weighs on them. This personalization is what turns a plain form into something people want to finish, share, and remember.

2

AI drafted the base, human expertise sharpened it

AI delivered the first draft: the 5 questions, the answer options, the 5 profile descriptions, the tone, the storytelling. Jocelyne and Gwenn then refined every line to ground the content in 40 years of real clinical expertise — that fine-tuning is what separates a generic AI quiz from one that actually lands. Estimated creation time, outside the beta phase: 1 to 1.5 hours. That's exactly the mechanic Tiquiz has built in: describe your topic in a few words, let the AI deliver the base, then personalize with what only you know.

3

Native Systeme.io integration, no tech headaches

For each result, Tiquiz applies a different tag to the contact directly in Systeme.io. That tag triggers a Systeme.io workflow that adds a generic "ADHD" tag and sends the email matched to the profile. No Zapier, no Make, not a single line of code. The integration is native — that's what makes setup accessible even for someone who's never touched the technical side.

4

A short email that does its job

The email stays short and delivers just the essentials: a brief intro to Jocelyne, a reminder of the profile so the reader can revisit it later, and a clean CTA to the book on Amazon. "Too much info kills the info," as Jocelyne puts it. Proof that you don't need to overcomplicate things to convert.

Your questions, the real answers

Does this work in every industry and every niche?

The quiz method is proven across dozens of industries: e-commerce (Warby Parker, Glossier), beauty (Sephora), media (BuzzFeed), B2C services (coaching, therapy, wellness), info products (courses, ebooks), B2B services (consulting, freelance). If your business has a topic where your prospect wants to better understand themselves or choose between several options, the quiz fits. Jocelyne's case is in personal development, but the mechanism — qualify, then recommend — works in any niche.

How long does it take to create a first quiz?

If you start from one of the 15 industry templates included in Tiquiz (coach, nutrition, parenting, finance, entrepreneur, photo, real estate, and others), budget 30 minutes the first time. If you start from a fully custom idea, budget 1 to 1.5 hours — that's the time Gwenn estimated for Jocelyne's quiz, outside the beta phase. The AI generates the draft, you personalize.

Do I have to run Meta ads to get results with a quiz?

No. Meta ads are what enabled Jocelyne to reach 285 leads in 9 days from zero audience. If you already have an audience (newsletter, community, social followers), you can start organically: share the quiz in your content, your posts, your emails. The volume will be more modest, but with no ad budget. You can add Meta ads later to accelerate if you want.

Can I do it all myself, without a freelancer, and still get results?

Yes, absolutely. Tiquiz is designed so you can create your quiz, connect it to Systeme.io, and launch a Meta ads campaign yourself. The Meta pixel installs in minutes, targeting happens through the standard Meta interface, and the mechanic stays the same. Your results will likely be more modest at the start than Jocelyne's (who benefits from Gwenn's continuous optimization experience), but you can definitely start on your own, and bring in a freelancer later if you want to scale.
