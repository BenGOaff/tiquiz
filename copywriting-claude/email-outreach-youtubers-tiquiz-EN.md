## Personalization notes

### The opening line

Has to prove you actually watched their video — not that someone forwarded you the link.

→ Good: `"I liked your hands-on approach, it's a refreshing change from the cookie-cutter tutorials"`
→ Weak: `"I love your content, it's super high quality"` (generic, copy-paste vibes)

### If you've already talked in their comments or stories

Skip the "I came across your video" opener. Go with: `"Hey [FIRST NAME], following our exchange under your video on [TOPIC]..."`

### If the creator is large (50K+ subscribers)

Add one line before the CTA: `"If your calendar's slammed, we can also block 15 minutes on a call — no strings attached."`

### Follow-up if no response (5-7 days later)

> Hey [FIRST NAME], quick follow-up. If "no time" or "not interested," just say so in one word and I'll stop writing. Otherwise just tell me which email address to send your access to and I'll fire it over 🙏

### Things to avoid

- ❌ Citing stats or sources (this is an email, not an article)
- ❌ "I hope this email finds you well"
- ❌ More than 4 links in the email
- ❌ Long paragraphs (1-2 sentences per block max)
- ❌ Hyped promises ("you'll make $5,000/month")
- ❌ Calling them "an influencer" (most creators hate the term)

### Variables to fill in

- `[FIRST NAME]` : double-checked on their bio
- `[VIDEO TOPIC]` : title of a video from the last 3 months max
- `[ANGLE / APPROACH / TAKE]` : a precise observation of their content
- `[THE GENERIC TUTORIALS / etc.]` : the "refreshing change from..." part must be honest

If you can't fill any of these honestly, don't send. 5 well-targeted creators >>> 50 spray-and-pray emails.
