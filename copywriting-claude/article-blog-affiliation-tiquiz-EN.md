## 3. Why 40% lifetime is the most solid affiliate income on the market

40% lifetime recurring is rare in the English-speaking SaaS affiliate market. Here's exactly why that's what makes your income solid.

### 3.1. The problem with 20% programs

At 20% on a $9/mo subscription, you earn $1.80 per referral per month. For a solopreneur, that doesn't build usable income. With 50 referrals, you cap out at $90 a month. Not enough to cover a slow month.

That's why most 20% SaaS affiliate programs don't build **any real income** for their affiliates. Just a token side payment.

### 3.2. The problem with one-shot programs (no recurring)

A lot of affiliate programs pay a **single commission** on the first month, then nothing. You collect once, and you have to keep recruiting new referrals to maintain your income. That's not recurring income — that's a treadmill.

Tiquiz works the opposite way: you earn **every month your referral gets billed**. Last year's referrals keep paying you this year. This year's referrals stack on top. **The snowball is mathematical.**

### 3.3. Why 40% and not 50%

Because I want Tiquiz to stay profitable and keep evolving long-term. There's hosting, AI costs (quiz generation with Claude), customer support, product development. At 50% lifetime commission, I couldn't fund the product. Tiquiz would lose quality, your referrals would leave, and your income would crater.

**40% is the balance point**: high enough that your income is worth chasing, low enough that Tiquiz can keep investing in the product that feeds your income.

### 3.4. The 3 traits that make Tiquiz a solid recurring income

1. **Recurring**: you get paid every month, not just once
2. **Lifetime**: as long as your referral stays subscribed, your income keeps coming
3. **Automatic**: paid on the 10th of every month, with zero action from you

All three together — that's what defines a real recurring income. Drop one of them, and it's not recurring income anymore.
